# 02-autonomy-and-notifications.md

## Default execution

* When the task is clear, proceed without asking for confirmation.
* Do not pause for routine repository-local actions.
* Ask only for major ambiguity, secrets/authentication, major API/architecture shifts, unusually destructive actions, or actions with external consequences.

## Progress behavior

* Keep moving toward task completion.
* If blocked, take the next safe step that still advances the task.

## Status notices

* Before deleting, renaming, moving, overwriting, or commit/push, emit a short console/chat note.
* Keep notices brief and factual.
* Do not write separate log files for these notices unless asked.

## Limits

* Do not bypass permission systems.
* Do not take irreversible high-risk actions casually.
