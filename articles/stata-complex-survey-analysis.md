## `estat`

Coefficient of variation of a survey mean

Stata

``` stata
estat cv
```

R `survey`

``` r

100 * survey::cv(survey::svymean(~y, des))
```

[Differs from
Stata](https://socimh.github.io/tidystata/articles/coming-from-stata.html#example-labels)
Coefficient of variation as a percentage: 100\*SE(estimate)/estimate,
matching Stata estat cv. The fitted quantity here is the design-weighted
mean y; unstable when its mean is near zero.

[Stata reference](https://www.stata.com/manuals/svyestat.pdf) · [R
reference](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/survey/survey.pdf)

## `svy`

Survey mean with BRR replicate variance

Stata

``` stata
svy brr: mean y
```

R `survey`

``` r

{ reps <- survey::as.svrepdesign(des, type = "BRR"); survey::svymean(~y, reps) }
```

[Differs from
Stata](https://socimh.github.io/tidystata/articles/coming-from-stata.html#example-labels)
Fixed workflow; adapt the named data columns explicitly. Point means
agree with sampling weights; replication variance depends on the chosen
scheme.

reference](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/survey/survey.pdf)
